Emits ultra-compact C99 bytecode packages linked natively with gcc or clang. Runs on any clean machine without installing Python or Node.

0.8ms Cold Startup

Sub-millisecond instant dispatch powered by the CVM 2.0 linear arena allocator. Eliminates serverless and CLI cold-start latency.
